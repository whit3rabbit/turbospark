# Spoken Language Identification (LID) Models

Language Identification (LID) models classify input audio into language IDs or provide posterior probabilities across spoken languages.

## Target Families & Upstream References

### 1. ECAPA-TDNN
- **Reference**: Emphasized Channel Attention, Propagation and Aggregation Time Delay Neural Network.
- **Capabilities**: Speaker recognition and multi-language spoken identification.

### 2. Wav2Vec2 LID
- **Reference**: Self-supervised multilingual speech representations with classification heads for 100+ languages.
