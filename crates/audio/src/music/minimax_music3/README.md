# minimax_music3

MiniMax Music 3: hierarchical autoregressive music generation with a
flow-matching latent decoder and a stereo 44.1 kHz vocoder.

## Reference

- Source: `mlx_audio/music/models/minimax_music3/` at mlx-audio
  commit
  [e1b19b9054bf163f5d812221a54fcc346f1890e9](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio/tree/e1b19b9054bf163f5d812221a54fcc346f1890e9/mlx_audio/music/models/minimax_music3)
  (version 0.5.7). The sibling checkout `../mlx-audio` is clean at
  that commit.
- Upstream attribution: the MLX implementation is adapted from
  [`mikolaj92/minimax-music3-mlx`](https://github.com/mikolaj92/minimax-music3-mlx)
  under Apache-2.0. MiniMax Music 3 weights and tokenizer are released
  under the MiniMax-Music3 Community License.
- Module map: `config`/constants and the pipeline live in `mod.rs`;
  `rng.rs` is the MLX-compatible sampler; `qwen3.rs` the AR backbone
  (time-major KV cache, in-place strided attention reads);
  `depth.rs` the RVQ residual decoder; `fusion.rs` the condition
  encoder; `dit.rs` + `euler.rs` the flow-matching stage; `vocoder.rs`
  the DAC-style decoder; `prompt.rs` the text frontend; `weights.rs`
  the loaders; `stress.rs` boundary and error-path gates; `timing.rs`
  and the `qwen3::bench` micro-bench the release-only timing profiles.

## Checkpoints

- Official modular tree (five component directories):
  `Model::load_official_tree` flattens the component configs, fuses
  vocoder `weight_g`/`weight_v` pairs, and remaps the PyTorch conv
  layouts (`[out, in, K]` and `[in, out, K]`) into the MLX
  `[out, K, in]` convention this crate's kernels convert from.
- Converted MLX trees (`mlx-community/MiniMax-Music3-*` layout:
  `config.json` plus safetensors shards, optionally sharded through
  `model.safetensors.index.json`): `Model::load_converted`. Affine
  2/3/4/6/8-bit linears dequantize through the shared crate kernel;
  `mxfp4`, `mxfp8`, and `nvfp4` modes are refused until verified here.
- The official repos (`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3`, and the
  `mlx-community` bf16/8bit/6bit/4bit/mxfp8/mxfp4/nvfp4 conversions)
  are documented pins, not installed or qualified locally; no digests
  are claimed. Real-checkpoint size (~11B parameters) places it
  outside this crate's local-install verification class.

## Text frontend

Official checkpoints need their Qwen tokenizer (the converted tree's
`tokenizer` directory), which this crate does not provide;
`Model::generate` consumes a pre-tokenized conditional id row (build
the text with `assemble_prompt` first), mirroring the reference's
`_encode_official_text_pair` contract. The deterministic tiny encoder
(`encode_tiny_text_pair`) covers local runs and fixtures.

## Verification

Fixtures live in `testdata/minimax_music3/` and are regenerated by
`tools/gen_minimax_music3_fixtures.py` from the pinned checkout (run
inside `../mlx-audio`; the conversion step fetches tokenizer metadata
from the hub). The manifest records SHA-256 digests and the generating
device, and lists only the files it digests. Regeneration was run
twice into clean trees on the same device: all content digests
reproduced byte-identically, and equal the checked-in fixtures.
`tools/gen_minimax_music3_bench_tree.py` generates the enlarged
synthetic trees used by the stress ceiling and the timing profiles
(see that script for variants and disk cost).

Gates (see `tests.rs`, `stress.rs`, `timing.rs`, and the
`qwen3::bench` micro-bench):

- RNG: threefry keys, split chains, uniforms, and categorical tokens
  are bit-exact against recorded MLX 0.32.3 draws across word-layout
  shapes; recorded decision margins prove no draw sits on a rounding
  knife-edge.
- Prompt: caption cleaning, lyric normalization, assembly, and the
  tiny text pair match the reference.
- AR: prefill hidden states and logits, per-frame sampled codebooks
  (token-exact over the recorded short and long traces), depth decoder
  step outputs, and feedback embeddings match.
- Flow: DiT velocities at fixed sigmas, per-chunk Euler latents with
  overlap carry (both chunks of a two-chunk schedule), the vocoder on
  long input, and the crop-stitched waveform match.
- End to end: the short scenario's full stereo waveform and a
  201-frame two-chunk scenario (tiled hiddens, recorded spots) match;
  the official-tree conversion path and an affine 8-bit tree load and
  generate.
- Contract: chunk scheduling, sigma schedule, crop constants, quant
  predicate, and the conv-transpose key matcher.
- Stress (`stress.rs`): valid boundaries (steps 1 and 30, a prompt at
  exactly MAX_PROMPT_TOKENS), empty-prompt refusal, chunk windows at
  200/201/300/301 (three chunks start at 301, two at 300),
  deterministic multi-chunk flow at all four, the end-token-first-frame
  zero-frames error (forced by patching the end-token lm_head row in a
  throwaway tree copy), `generate()` completing when the end token
  stops the AR stage early, and `#[ignore]`d release-only runs to the
  9000-frame ceiling over the committed `tiny_long` bench tree.
- Timing (`timing.rs`, `qwen3::bench`): reporting only, release only,
  one test thread. Tiny end-to-end RTF, AR scaling to the frame
  ceiling, multi-chunk flow, a per-piece frame anatomy, real-width AR
  and real-DiTs flow profiles over the synthetic bench trees, and the
  rope/KV/attention micro-bench. Rows are recorded in `MODELS.md`;
  CPU numbers are never Metal or quality claims.
- Mutation checks were applied to the subtle guards (the time-major
  KV append, per-channel stitch, per-channel overlap capture, DiT
  input transpose, short-chunk crop guard, remap dims, sigma divisor,
  RNG word layout) and every mutation was caught. The KV-append check
  was re-run after the layout change: dropping the unconditional
  row's append fails the AR parity gate immediately.

## Divergences

- `rng::normal` evaluates `erfinv` through an f64 Acklam inverse-normal
  approximation rather than Metal's `erfinv`; the uniform stream is
  bit-exact and draws agree to f32 rounding, but `mx.random.normal`
  values are not guaranteed bit-identical. The flow stage's initial
  noise therefore matches distributionally, not bitwise; parity tests
  replay recorded noise where exactness matters.
- The official-tree loader accepts optional token-contract fields
  (`audio_code_offset`, `audio_end_token_id`, `audio_cfg_token_id`,
  `semantic_vocab_size`) in the language-model config. The reference
  pins these to official constants that assume a 200k vocab; a small
  vocab needs explicit values to stay self-consistent, so the fixture
  tree carries them.

## Open gates

Real-checkpoint parity, quality, runtime/catalog/FFI/Swift
integration, and the official tokenizer port are unqualified; see
`MODELS.md` for the pinned-profile rules those gates require. The
CPU timing profiles bound this port's software path only: at real
backbone widths an AR frame costs seconds on CPU (measured; the depth
decoder's seven sequential expansions and the per-layer GEMVs
dominate), so real-checkpoint use waits on the Metal offload, which is
its own separately gated optimization. The real-dims flow profile and
the tiny scenarios' rows are recorded in `MODELS.md`.
